Between the lines

What football taught me

Three lessons, none of them about winning.

Adversity is fuel.

Before the CIF championship my junior year, I thought about how far I'd come: three broken collarbones, a transfer and earning a starting spot. Adversity gave me motivation no one else had.

Not everything goes your way. Deal with it.

A game, like life, never goes exactly to plan. The response is what matters.

It's more than a game. It's a brotherhood.

You play for the guy next to you, and high school football is where I've felt that bond most deeply.
