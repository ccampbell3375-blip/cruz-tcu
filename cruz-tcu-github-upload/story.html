Chapter one

My Story

The short version of how a kid from Carlsbad ended up wanting to run through TCU's campus. Scroll down. It's a timeline.

Elementary school · Where it started

Friday Night Lights, down the street

My earliest football memory: playing on the field just down the street from our house, with my dad as the coach and all my elementary school buddies as teammates.

8th grade to 2024 · First time I got knocked down

Three collarbones

I've broken my collarbone three times: twice on the left, once on the right. The first was in eighth grade. I figured it was a simple break and everything would be fine.

Going into freshman year in 2023, I was excited for the season. In one catch, my collarbone snapped in half. In 2024, going into sophomore year, it felt the worst. I'd trained all offseason, and in our first full-pads practice I fell wrong on top of the ball. I knew that pain right away.

Standing on the sideline, watching my team and not playing, was one of the hardest things I've done. It taught me patience and perseverance.

Summer before junior year · Starting over

Nobody knew my name

I transferred from Carlsbad to Santa Fe Christian. At Carlsbad, people knew what I could do. At SFC, nobody knew my name. I spent the early summer putting in the work, rep after rep, until I earned the starting wide receiver spot. Today I'm scoring touchdowns for that same team.

Santa Fe Christian · Turning point

Mr. Kim and the word "why"

My Bible teacher, Mr. Kim, is one of my biggest mentors. He taught me to stop constantly asking "why" and to place my trust in God instead. That shift became a turning point for me. My faith has shaped my happiness and my understanding of who I am.

This fall · The sweatpants kid

A dad I'd never met

This fall, the dad of a player I had competed against at a football camp came up to me and asked, "Were you the sweatpants kid?" I'd worn sweatpants to one of the camps. He told me I was one of the best kids there. It's amazing how something like that can stick with you and make your day, your week, even your month.

Now · Senior year

Ready for what's next.

I want an environment that challenges me and lets me focus my learning on the career I'm building toward in finance and real estate. In return, I'll bring energy, a team-first mindset and the ability to connect with anyone.
